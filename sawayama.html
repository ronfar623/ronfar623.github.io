GAME RULES BY ZACHTRONICS
  • Every tableau card is dealt face up. Only top cards or proper descending, alternating-color runs can be moved.
  • Any card (or run) may fill an empty column — not just kings.
  • The deck is drawn three at a time, one pass only. It never reshuffles.
  • When the deck runs out, its slot becomes a free cell holding one card.
  • Foundation cards are final — they never come back down.
  • There's no auto-mover: the computer never places a card for you. Drag it onto a foundation — or double-click it — to send it up. Miss your chance, and that's part of the challenge.
